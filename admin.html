PAYMENT GATEWAY

Square Appointments & Checkout

The current Square booking page is the live booking and checkout route. Square supports online card payments and eligible digital wallets; the exact options depend on the merchant account, country, and checkout configuration.

Open Square Dashboard ↗

Configure card acceptance, prepayment, cancellation/no-show policy and tips in the Square merchant dashboard. Recommended tip presets: 20%, 25%, 30%, with custom/No Tip where Square checkout supports it. Verify the live customer checkout before advertising these options.

OTHER PAYMENT OPTIONS

PayPal · Venmo · Zelle

These are not yet connected to a tested website checkout. PayPal and Venmo require a verified merchant integration or payment link; Zelle is generally a manual bank-transfer method, not an embedded card gateway. Keep them hidden as online checkout choices until the owner confirms and tests the actual payment destination.
