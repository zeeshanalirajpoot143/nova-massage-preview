Privacy & Cookie Policy

This page explains the information NOVA MASSAGE may receive when you use this website. The studio should review and approve this policy before the website is made public.

Information you provide

When you submit an appointment request, callback request, contact form, or review, the information you enter may include your name, email address, phone number, preferred appointment details, message, and review. These details are used to respond to your request and manage studio enquiries.

Booking and payments

When you continue to Square, booking availability, appointment confirmation, and any payment information are handled on Square's platform and are subject to Square's own privacy terms. NOVA MASSAGE's website does not ask you to enter card details into its own forms.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses browser storage for essential preferences, such as remembering your selected display theme and your cookie-notice choice. The current website version does not intentionally load advertising or analytics trackers. External services you choose to open, such as Square, WhatsApp, or externally hosted fonts, may process technical information under their own policies.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to respond to enquiries, review appointment requests, manage customer communications, and maintain the website's services. Do not submit payment-card details or sensitive personal information through general website forms.

Data requests and contact

For questions about a request you submitted or to ask about your information, contact NOVA MASSAGE at thenovamassage@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +1 (708) 400-9333.

This is a website-policy draft, not legal advice. The studio owner must confirm the actual data-retention practices, service providers, and legal requirements for the studio's location before launch.
